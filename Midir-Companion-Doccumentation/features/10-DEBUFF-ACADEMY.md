# Debuff Academy

**Applies to:** Midir Companion 0.3.11d.

**Scope:** Current implemented behavior only. Historical changes are recorded in [`CHANGELOG.md`](../CHANGELOG.md).

## Learning modes

- Offers a field guide to nine effect categories, introductory lessons, flash cards and recognition challenges (including “New Debuff, Who This?”).
- Supports effect-icon and category learning; the content is intentionally distinct from imported combat-report proof.

## Progress and accuracy

- Practice feedback reflects the curated local registry. When imported reports do not contain an effect observation, absence of proof must not be treated as proof of a debuff application.
