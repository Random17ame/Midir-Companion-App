# Arcana field guides

**Applies to:** Midir Companion 0.3.4.

**Scope:** Current implemented behavior only. Historical changes are recorded in [`CHANGELOG.md`](../CHANGELOG.md).

## Current scope

- The Arcana Simulators landing area includes field-guide material across ten Arcana choices and links to relevant practice modes. The descriptions are educational prompts and local training references, not imported combat events.
- Skill icons and character artwork are drawn from bundled local registries where available.

## Evidence distinction

- Skill descriptions, cooldown models and simulator behaviors should not be confused with actor-verified report evidence or current live-game balance. Rotation Highlights applies separate recorded-evidence constraints.
