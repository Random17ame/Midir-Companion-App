# Ogham Runes — Inventory, Five-Socket Arcana Loadouts and Practice Rerolls (0.3.15 draft)

**Implementation:** `Companion Files/ogham-runes.js`, `Companion Files/ogham-effect-levels.js`, `Companion Files/app.js`, `Companion Files/styles.css`. Ogham data is stored under the existing `state.ogham` profile field and travels with normal profile backups. It is not applied automatically to damage, Rotation Highlights, healing, Oath or achievement math.

## Owned Rune Inventory and Pentagonal Equipment Board

1. Open **Arcana Simulators → Ogham Rune Inventory**. **Add Rune** opens a visual gallery of all **26 rune words** in game-inspired icon tiles, grouped into five Special and twenty-one Regular words. Select a rune stone by click or touch, then set Fine/Exquisite/Masterwork grade and 1/2/3 effect lines appropriate to its grade. Each effect uses a compact level selector with its numeric bonus displayed directly below, not inside the selector. Legacy nickname/notes are preserved in saved data but removed from the compact editor. The chosen stone remains visible in the editor, and **Change Rune** reopens the same gallery rather than presenting a word dropdown. Changes are drafted until **Save**; cancelling the gallery or editor leaves owned inventory and previously saved rune instances unchanged. Special key rune words are Masterwork-only. Each owned instance has a stable internal ID; it can be edited and removed without mutating other instances.
2. Choose an Arcana and an Awakening Level **0–50**, saved separately for each Arcana. Five sockets are arranged in a **pentagon**, following the in-game arrangement; sockets unlock at Awakening Levels **1, 5, 10, 15 and 20**. **Levels 21–50 remain selectable and persist but do not unlock additional sockets or change the current Ogham combination calculations.** **16 clearly labeled stones** (Sail, Dair, Uilleand, Gétal, Coll, Straiph, Óir, Tinne, Fearn, Luis, Cert, Peithe, Gort, Onn, Ailm and Nin) use locally bundled, individually clipped user-supplied screenshots of the **in-game slab silhouettes and tilted markings**. The **other ten words** use centered, provisional local stone artwork reconstructed from the existing slab silhouettes, colors and approximate Ogham marks; these are not verified game images. Beithe uses the orange special-stone style. The same artwork is used in the visual picker, owned inventory, pentagon sockets and selected-rune details. Local image mapping can replace individual fallbacks as higher-resolution screenshots are provided. The included image sprites are referenced only by their stable word IDs and bundled offline.
3. **Equip by tap or drag:** Tap an owned rune in the visual inventory, then tap a socket. On desktop, dragging an inventory stone to a socket also equips it. A rune may appear only once per selected Arcana loadout. Drag an equipped rune to another socket, or tap the first socket then the second, to move/swap it. Dragging a socket's rune back into the inventory region unequips it.
4. **Tap occupied sockets twice:** The *first* click/touch on an occupied socket selects that rune, highlights its socket, and opens a small detail strip beneath the board with its identity, grade, effect names, levels, bonus values and any notes. A *second* click/touch **on that same socket** unequips it from the Arcana while preserving the rune in inventory. No extra X or unequip control appears on sockets. Selecting another rune/socket clears the pending same-socket unequip action. Slot actions preserve the existing save and scroll-position behavior.
5. Different Arcana have independent saved loadouts. A physical instance can be referenced by several saved Arcana loadouts, but only the currently active Arcana's equipped effects are relevant in the game. Changing the chosen Arcana in Companion displays its saved loadout; it does not change the Arcana in the game.

### Combinations and recommended equip

The rule database includes **26 rune words and 30 three-word recipes** (10 Arcana × 3, including Korean-live/December NA planning recipes). The key word must be a Masterwork special rune worth 3 points; two other required words contribute Fine 1, Exquisite 2 or Masterwork 3 points. The required three give **5–9 points**. Two extra equipped runes do not raise that recipe's points. The expandable **Combinations ⓘ** and **Recommended equip ⓘ** sections give discreet hints about activation and missing words rather than filling the equipment board with details. Numeric damage/skill-shape changes from synergized combinations are *not* applied to other Companion calculations.


## Persistent rune collections and organization (0.3.12k)

The tracker has **two separate, always visible collections** using different stable instance IDs:

1. **Tracked / owned runes** represent manually entered items or the player's actual in-game runes. Add, edit and delete remain independent of all simulator rerolls.
2. **Simulated runes** are saved reroll results created by **Save as New** in EV → Reforges → Ogham. This never duplicates or modifies a tracked rune. A saved simulated rune can be **loaded back** into the Ogham simulator; subsequent rerolls change only the simulator copy. **Save as New** stores a second instance, while **Update Saved Rune** explicitly writes over the loaded simulated instance. Simulated runes can also be edited, grouped, favorited and removed in their own inventory.

Both collections equip into **the same five Arcana pentagon sockets**, and both participate equally in the saved Arcana loadout, completed-synergy recognition, summed effect lines, and compare tool. Deleting a rune instance clears any equipment and comparison slots containing its ID. Each inventory preserves independent manual order, and source collection remains visible in the UI. Both are retained in existing browser profile backups (same profile key, not a separate browser wallet).

The search field matches rune word, grade, older saved nickname/notes, optional visual-container caption, and **effect line names in English, Korean and Spanish**. Typing `Magnum` prioritizes runes with Magnum Shot effects; nonmatches stay visible, subdued, and equipable. Favorites and sorting (Manual/Favorites/Grade/Name/Group/Recent) remain available. A rune can be repositioned without changing its saved ID or Arcana equipment.

**Visual grouping (0.3.12l):** Use **New Group** once to create an unnamed container in either collection. Drop a rune directly **onto the center of another rune** to combine them into a group, or drop at its edge to reorder. Drop into an existing group or select a rune and tap the group to add it; groups can be reordered as units. On touch, drag gestures use the same target semantics. The group outline supports optional naming by double-click/double-tap, with a small caption only when named. The × appears only while the container is hovered, focused or selected; it **dissolves the group, never deletes its runes**. Moving a rune out removes only its group membership. Group contents remain visible rather than becoming separate folders or hidden collections. Old free-text group labels migrate into named containers when saved state is loaded.

**Compact inventory (0.3.12r):** Each collection uses a dense grid of Rune artwork, word/name, and a small equipped-slot marker. Grade and attribute information remain available in the editor and the compact frame color (Fine blue, Exquisite purple, Masterwork gold, ungraded gray); effect search still highlights matching lines. The star, pencil and × are favorite, edit and remove; simulated tiles also retain an icon to load for reroll. Drag the stone to a center to group or to an edge to reorder; drag group handles to move groups. The visible ordering arrows are removed. In Custom order, Alt + Up/Down on an ungrouped rune or group handle is the keyboard alternative. On touch, swipe the label or non-artwork area to scroll; drag from the stone. Tracked and simulated collections scroll independently as needed, with desktop right column capped at 590px, and each collection and inline compare chooser capped at 282px (260px at phone widths). Selection, edit and favorites preserve internal scroll offsets; searching starts at the top. No stored identity or equipment rules change.

## Equipped effects and non-destructive Set A / Set B comparison (0.3.12k)

The expandable **Equipped Effects** panel aggregates every currently slotted rune line for the selected Arcana. It groups the same effect ID and bonus unit, adds the discrete-level values, and separately lists completed recipes with grade points and region/availability status. Unknown values are explicitly marked, not converted to zero bonuses silently. These are reported attribute line totals, **not** predicted final combat damage or healing.

The expandable **Compare 5-Rune Sets** section always takes **Set A directly from the currently equipped Arcana**. A cannot be edited there. **Set B initially mirrors Set A** so there is no second setup from scratch; it becomes an independently persisted per-Arcana configuration only after the user customizes it or chooses to clear/copy it. If A changes later, the comparison immediately reflects the new A, while a customized B remains intact. **Reset B to equipped** copies the current A again.

Set B accepts runes from **either collection** and may reference the same physical rune instances already in A. It prevents repeating a rune *within B*, but allows shared runes in A and B, in matching or different positions. The UI highlights unchanged, added, removed, moved, or replaced sockets, displays the number of shared rune IDs, shows increased/decreased attribute totals as **B minus A** (matching only effect ID and units), and highlights changed synergy points/activation. It never equips Set B or modifies inventory items. Effect values reconstructed from Nexon's endpoints retain the caveat in Guide 23; no damage multiplier or hypothetical synergy skill behavior is inferred.

## Effect levels: 97 source-described types

The offline catalog includes **52 general Talent effects** and **45 special/Arcana effects**, extracted from the English Nexon NA November 2026 update, with their published minimum and maximum values. Talent effects have 10 or 20 levels; Arcana effects have 10. In the manual inventory editor, choose an *integer* level and see the numeric bonus beneath the controls; the level dropdown contains just the level number. In EV Rune mode, each randomly rolled line instead shows its read-only effect name, bonus and actual level / maximum.

**Evidence limit:** The source patch gives the endpoints but not every intermediate value. The bundled `Values` arrays reconstruct intervening tiers with equal increments where supported by those endpoints; they are **not** a directly captured Prilus `OghamAbilityList.Values` dump. Legacy saved profiles with a previously imported, validated Prilus-style catalog continue to honor that data for backward compatibility. There is **no player-facing JSON importer** in the Rune simulator. Stable IDs `src:90001`–`src:90097` are Companion-local and do not pretend to be official game effect IDs. See feature guide `23-OGHAM-EFFECT-LEVELS.md` for transcription and migration rules. The reconstructed pool breadth is substantially better than the old 18-effect draft but should **not** be advertised as authoritative live roll odds.

Regular words roll only general effects; special words roll from the full general + Arcana pool, regardless of any Arcana equipment selection. Effects may not duplicate within one rune. Each available effect and its level are sampled uniformly, following the reviewed Prilus algorithm.

## EV simulator / shared practice chests

In **Explosive Vanguard → Reforge Simulator**, choose **Rune** from the single **Item Type** selector. This replaces the previous three mode buttons. Choosing Rune opens the same visual 26-word picker used by the inventory; ordinary equipment reforge and Echostone remain alternative Item Types and retain their existing mechanics. The reroll simulator has **no Arcana selector**: all rune words and eligibility pools are shared, and any rune may be planned for any Arcana. The Arcana selector belongs only to the tracker, where each Arcana has a **different saved five-slot equipment loadout** using the same two shared inventories.

Fine has one effect and no locks, Exquisite has two effects and at most one lock, and Masterwork has three effects and at most two locks. Effect names and levels in the **reroll simulator are read-only random results**, not player-selectable dropdowns; only the rune word/grade and line-lock controls affect the roll setup. **Start Over** clears the current practice rune and its per-rune spending history, without charging the chest. The reroll button shows the *next roll* gold and herb costs beside it; changing the number of locked lines updates the price immediately. **Zero locked lines cost 5,000 Gold only** (no herbs, no Herb Chest requirement). Locking one or two lines adds a Companion-only herb surcharge. Every paid reroll validates all required balances up front, charging Gold from the EV Storage Chest and herbs from Herb Collection only when sufficient. Herb cost, spending and Disassemble preview entries reuse the gathering sprite catalog. The **same full Storage Chest** visible during ordinary reforges also appears in Rune mode, with live Gold, Ducat, tool, item and Herb balances. Rune mode omits reforge-only item purchase/transfer actions. Balances update immediately after reroll spending and Disassemble credits. Simulated rerolls never modify tracked inventory, saved simulated instances or Arcana equipment without explicit Save as New / Update Saved Rune.

**Visual result cards (November 25):** Each effect has its own bordered row with a 44px open/closed lock button, localized action label, and `aria-pressed` state. Locked rows use a slate border without dimming effect text; 9–10/10 and 18–20/20 rolls retain gold text even when locked. The threshold is 90% of the actual discrete maximum (rounded upward); a maximum roll receives an additional subtle inset outline. Empty lines and unavailable locks remain disabled. No roll rules or effect values change.

### Per-rune history and Disassemble (0.3.12l)

Each practice rune records its own **reroll count**, cumulative Gold and per-herb spending; the old simulator-wide total is no longer used as the cost/refund ledger. **Save as New** creates a simulated inventory copy with its own ledger. Loading a saved simulated rune for more rolls does not silently overwrite it; **Update Saved Rune** commits the changes, while Disassemble can use the current uncommitted spending when the loaded rune is disassembled. Tracked/manual runes cannot be disassembled using this practice-refund action.

The **Disassemble** action requires confirmation and credits a practice refund of **1–10% of that rune's own cumulative paid reroll spending**; the percentage increases with spending according to the modeled practice scale. Gold and Herb refunds are rounded up to whole units when nonzero; an unused rune with zero spending returns zero. Disassembling a saved simulated rune removes that instance from the simulated inventory and clears its equipment/compare references, leaving all other runes unchanged. An unsaved active rune with paid rerolls may also be disassembled. **Disassemble is a Companion practice mechanic, not a claim about real game refunds.**

The effect JSON uploader, debug catalog status, and Prilus development link have been removed from the player-facing Rune reroll screen. The 97-effect bundled catalog still loads offline; existing profile imports are read to preserve saved data, but future catalog corrections should be made in the source.


## Language and appearance

Controls, word-picker headings and socket instructions are localized separately for **English, Korean and Spanish**, using stable rune, recipe and effect IDs underneath. Confirmed English patch names are used in English; incomplete Korean and Spanish translations use neutral localized placeholders rather than fake confirmed game names. The third synergy English names remain working translations pending NA release. Sixteen in-game rune silhouettes and their stroke shapes were extracted from the **user-provided rune inventory screenshot**, not inferred from Unicode lettering. The remaining ten use provisional, reconstructed PNG stone art in `assets/ogham/provisional/` (Beithe, Edad, Ebhadh, Emancholl, Idad, Iphin, Muin, Ruis, Úath and Úr). The image tiles are low-resolution references awaiting higher-resolution per-word screenshots; decorative frames and grade outlines are Companion-created. On desktop widths, the tracked and simulated inventories render beside the equipped five-socket board to shorten drag distance; on mobile they return to the stacked flow. The Set A/Set B comparison tool now chooses runes from an inline inventory directly below the compare slot rows rather than from a separate overlay. Equipped socket runes intentionally omit the extra inner border used elsewhere so the socket frame remains the primary border. Do not import third-party art without confirming its source and reuse terms. `window.MIDIR_OGHAM_ICONS` supports later user-provided *local* image assets.
